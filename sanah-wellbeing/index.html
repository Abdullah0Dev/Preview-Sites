Real support for real feelings.

Navigating life, work, and everything in between can get heavy really fast. If you're dealing with stress, feeling anxious, or just overwhelmed by everyday life, you don't have to figure it all out alone.

Here is how we can work together:

Tailored to you

No rigid scripts or one-size-fits-all advice. We focus on whatever you're going through right now, using tools that actually fit your situation.

Real support for real feelings

My goal is to help you process painful thoughts, manage stress, and build a life that actually feels good to live.

A space where you can be yourself

As a black woman, I know how rare and important it is to find a space where you feel truly heard. Whether you've never had room to share your story or just need a place to let your guard down, this is for you.

100% confidential and judgment-free

What you share stays between us. When you don't have to worry about being judged, you can finally talk through the hard stuff freely.

Therapy isn't just for crises—it's for anyone who wants a bit more clarity, peace, and support. Whenever you're ready, I'm here to help.
