ABOUT DHR CONSULTANCY

HR is people. And people are business.

I'm Dione, a CIPD-qualified HR professional with 18+ years' experience including hands-on work stabilising HR in businesses with no existing systems in place.

I help small and growing businesses get the HR basics right; from hiring, contracts, policies to managing staff issues without the cost or complexity of a full-time HR hire.

Many business owners handle HR themselves alongside everything else, and it's usually fine until something comes up they're not sure how to handle: a difficult conversation with an employee, a contract question, or simply knowing what they're legally required to do.

Through DHR Consultancy, I provide practical, straightforward support on a flexible, project or ongoing basis.
