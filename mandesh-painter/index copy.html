Common questions

Anything not covered here, just call or message.

How long does a room take?

An average bedroom with walls only is usually one day. Add ceilings and woodwork and it's closer to two. Wallpaper stripping or heavy filling can add a day, and you'll be told that up front rather than halfway through.

Do you supply the paint?

Yes. Trade paint is included in the quoted price, and you pick the colours. If you'd rather buy your own or you've already got a specific brand in mind, that's no problem — the price is adjusted accordingly.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. Furniture is moved to the middle of the room and covered, and floors are sheeted before anything starts. If you'd like to clear small or delicate items yourself beforehand, that always helps.

Are you insured?

Yes — £2m public liability cover. A copy of the certificate is available on request, and it's often needed for flats and managed buildings.

How do I pay, and when?

Bank transfer or cash, paid on completion once you're happy with the work. Larger jobs can be split into stage payments agreed before the start. No deposit is asked for on standard room work.

How soon can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks, and sometimes sooner for smaller jobs or cancellations. Message with your postcode and what you need, and you'll get the next available dates.

What if I'm not happy with something?

We walk round together before the job is signed off, so anything you spot gets sorted there and then. Workmanship is guaranteed for two years afterwards.
