Our Process

A Clear Path From Idea to Completion

Clearly, efficiently, and with investor goals in mind — every step of the way.

01

Free Consultation & Site Visit

We begin with a no-obligation consultation and, where applicable, a site visit to understand your project goals and investment strategy.

02

Architectural Proposal

A clear, transparent proposal outlining project brief, design stages, required services, timeline and fees — before any commitment.

03

Measured Survey

A detailed measured survey of the property to capture accurate dimensions and existing structural elements — ensuring precision from the ground up.

04

Design Development

Full architectural drawings including floor plans, elevations and sections. Optional 3D visualisations available for planning and investor confidence.

05

Client Review & Revisions

A full review session via site meeting, video call or phone. We apply revisions carefully to ensure everything aligns with your goals.
