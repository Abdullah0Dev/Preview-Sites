ABOUT DANIELLE

Strategy. Creativity. Real impact.

I'm a brand strategist running my own consultancy, helping founders, CEO's and organisations turn what they do into brands that people actually understand, trust and buy into.

My entrepreneurial streak started at 8 when I convinced my teachers to let me launch (and sell) a school magazine and I've been building things ever since.

Over the last 15 years I've worked across brand, marketing and creative contributing to rebrands and marketing strategies that have delivered measurable growth, including +30% profit increases. I led the brand strategy for Hospital Rooms, shaping the foundation for a relaunch that resulted in +105% users and a +542% increase in donate page views.

Alongside my consultancy, I co-founded The ReSet Board an integrated unit of brand, sales, operations and leadership specialists working as one board for growing businesses. I'm a visiting lecturer at Birmingham City University, BIMM and Loughborough, I host the Build Better Brands podcast, where I share practical thinking on how to build brands that actually work.
