About Tara

Designing Spaces for a More Considered Life.


I'm an interior designer offering full design services for residential and commercial projects. I do everything from design concept to installation, working collaboratively with trades to deliver high end finishes. I save clients the time required and stress inevitably involved with renovation or new build projects.

I design with a collaborative approach as I believe interiors should serve and support those who use it. Rather than being trend-led, I am a nature-led designer.

I offer stand alone packages for clients who only need home staging, design consultation or technical drawings for layout planning.
