ABOUT SAIF

A more confident financial future, together.

I help individuals and business owners build, protect and preserve their wealth through clear, personalised financial planning.

My approach starts with understanding your financial circumstances, goals and priorities without judgement, before creating a plan designed to make your finances simpler today while building towards the future.

I'm particularly passionate about helping people who have worked hard to create wealth ensure that their money is structured effectively, protected appropriately and positioned to support the next generation.

I'm also keen to build strong professional relationships with accountants, business owners and other trusted advisers where we can work together to deliver better outcomes for our clients.
